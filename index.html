Strategy

Four priorities guide everything we do.

Crush violent crime.

The FBI will destroy the ability of violent actors, gangs, and cartels to operate. The FBI will dismantle criminal enterprises.

Defend the homeland.

The FBI will protect the United States from terrorist attack, foreign intelligence espionage, foreign cyber operations, and adversarial cybercriminal activity.

Rebuild public trust.

The FBI will restore trust with the American people by showing accountability, professionalism, and transparency in every operation.

Fierce organizational accountability.

The FBI will hold leadership and personnel accountable to the highest standards of conduct, efficiency, and performance.

Partnerships

Our partnerships strengthen public safety nationwide.

Join task forces

Task forces unite federal, state, and local law enforcement agencies to share intelligence, conduct investigations, and coordinate arrests in terrorism, gang, cybercrime, crimes against children, and human trafficking cases.

Services and data

The FBI's Criminal Justice Information Services Division, or CJIS, is a high-tech hub that provides a range of state-of-the-art tools and services, including crime data and tip processing to law enforcement.

Training and professional development

The FBI offers training opportunities to U.S. and international agencies and partners. The range of professional courses includes the National Academy.

Crisis response

The Critical Incident Response Group (CIRG) is responsible for the Bureau's global readiness and response to the world's most complex, high-consequence threats.

Cyber

The success of the FBI Cyber mission depends on our partnerships with law enforcement, intelligence agencies, and the private sector.

Law enforcement engagement

The FBI supports its large network of law enforcement partners, including federal, state, local, tribal, territorial, and campus agencies through engagements and trainings.
