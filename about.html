About the FBI

Protecting the American People. Upholding the Constitution.

The Federal Bureau of Investigation is the principal investigative arm of the U.S. Department of Justice and a member of the U.S. Intelligence Community. Through intelligence gathering, criminal investigations, and partnerships with law enforcement agencies, the FBI works to protect the American people and safeguard national security.

Our purpose

Our Mission

To protect the American people and uphold the Constitution of the United States.

The Bureau investigates federal crimes, identifies national security threats, gathers intelligence, and works with law enforcement partners to protect the country. Its work is carried out under the law and in service to the public.

Our work

What We Do

CT

Counterterrorism

Detect and disrupt terrorist threats, investigate acts of terrorism, and work with partners to protect the public.

CI

Counterintelligence

Identify and counter foreign intelligence activity and espionage that threaten U.S. interests.

CY

Cybercrime

Investigate cyber intrusions and other malicious online activity, coordinating response with public and private partners.

PC

Public Corruption

Investigate corruption involving public officials and the abuse of public trust.

CR

Civil Rights

Enforce federal civil rights laws and investigate violations, including hate crimes and color-of-law abuses.

VC

Violent Crime

Investigate violent criminal activity and work with local, state, federal, and international partners.

TO

Transnational Organized Crime

Target criminal networks that operate across borders and threaten communities and national security.

FC

Financial Crime

Investigate significant fraud, money laundering, and other complex financial crimes.

WMD

Weapons of Mass Destruction

Prevent, detect, and respond to threats involving chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear materials.

Since 1908

Our History

1908

A mission that has grown with the nation

Established in 1908, the Bureau developed from a small investigative force into a national investigative and intelligence agency. As the country and its security challenges changed, the FBI's work expanded across federal law enforcement, intelligence gathering, and partnerships with agencies at home and abroad.

How we serve

Our Values
